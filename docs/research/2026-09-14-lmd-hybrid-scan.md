Method: a title is listed when it contains "doku" (case-insensitive). A title match is a
candidate, not a verified hybrid: some are sudoku-tagged puzzles that merely mention a genre,
and hybrids whose title omits "sudoku" are missed. Tags with fewer than 60 puzzles were read
in full; for larger tags only the newest 60 were read, so older hybrids are not listed.
