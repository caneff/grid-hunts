Renbanana lineup

Every candidate grid found so far. Chocolate groups are rectangles whose orthogonal neighbours differ by at least 5; banana groups are non-rectangles holding distinct consecutive digits. A circle marks a cell whose digit equals the size of its own group — dark on chocolate, green on banana — a clue the puzzle could offer, not one that has been chosen. A banana group is a renban holding a run of k consecutive digits, so a circle needs the run to start at or below k — which it always does once the group has five cells or more. Those circles are forced by the group's size alone, rule out no shading, and are drawn dashed and hidden by default. Hover a cell to light up its whole group.
