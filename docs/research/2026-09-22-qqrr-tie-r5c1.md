## Channelled retries

The retry pass was replaced before it launched. `.scratch/place/chan_sweep.py`
posts the tie digit by digit: each window rank gets tens and units variables
tied to its width literal, a task fixes which window of each cell is one digit
wide (12 slot patterns per pair, different slots), and the two 7-digit
sequences are equated element-wise. On the tr pair r4c2 = r5c3, a 180 s
timeout under the plain model, 11 of 12 patterns were proven infeasible in
76 s on 4 processes, 6 of them in 0.1 s. Soundness: the first bl grid fixed
under the channelled model is accepted in its slot pattern (r7c2 narrow first,
r7c5 narrow last) and rejected in others, instantly. The retries run over each
corner's timed-out pairs, 6 processes, 180 s per task
(`.scratch/place/scheduler2.sh`); tallies go here as they finish.

### Solver settings, measured

Three channelled tasks that timed out at 180 s in the tr retry (r4c2 = r5c3
slots 30, r5c3 = r5c5 slots 03, r5c3 = r5c6 slots 02), each run under six
settings with a 180 s cap (`.scratch/place/measure.py`, `measure.log`):

| setting | task 1 | task 2 | task 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| default, 1 worker | timeout | timeout | timeout |
| linearization 2, 1 worker | timeout | timeout | timeout |
| no hint + tens tables, 1 worker | timeout | timeout | timeout |
| no hint + tables + lin2, 1 worker | infeasible 159 s | timeout | timeout |
| default, 6 workers | infeasible 60 s | infeasible 147 s | infeasible 85 s |
| no hint + tables + lin2, 6 workers | infeasible 37 s | infeasible 108 s | infeasible 63 s |

The portfolio is the lever: 6 workers prove all three where 1 worker proves at
most one. The flags add roughly a third on top. A proof retry should run one
task at 6 workers per pool, not six tasks at 1 worker; the big finder already
runs 6 workers with the tables flag.
